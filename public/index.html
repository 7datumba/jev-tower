method & caveats (all numbers from recorded runs) Geometry (closest point of approach) is computed deterministically from live tracks. Ground truth in comparisons is the deterministic CPA physics rule (lateral CPA under 3 NM AND vertical under 1000 ft within 120 s), not aviation outcomes. This measures separation-standard margins, not crash probability - real ATC is flying these planes.

Conflict probability (the physics %): analytic trajectory-uncertainty method after Paielli & Erzberger, "Conflict Probability Estimation for Free Flight", NASA 1997 (open: ntrs.nasa.gov 19970001686). Assumptions printed for honesty: per-aircraft position sigma grows 1.0 NM/min along-track and 0.4 NM/min cross-track from 0.05 NM nav error; vertical gate softened to a logistic around 1000 ft. The deterministic CPA gates match the approach used by the open-source BlueSky ATM simulator (TUDelft-CNS-ATM/bluesky).

Tower comparison, identical 60-tick recorded window: Jev watch layer 103 ms avg, flagged every real conflict (0 missed of 385), 335 of 720 flags false against the physics rule. gpt-4o-mini: 2,242 ms (22x slower), missed 160 of 385. gpt-4o footnote: 2,356 ms, missed 56 of 385 - bigger models are more careful but not faster, which is the point of a judgment model in a 1 Hz loop.

False-positive reduction, tuned on the recorded window: threshold sweeps and multi-tick persistence could not cut false alarms without creating misses (Jev's watch probabilities are deliberately broad). Letting Jev audit itself with the exact CPA numbers and rule in the prompt still missed 180 of 385 real losses - a model is the wrong tool for verification. So the separation vet is deterministic code: raw model flags (watch p above 50%) are triage; a conflict is only confirmed, and a reroute only issued, when the CPA rule itself fires. Final system on the recorded window: 720 raw flags, 385 vet-confirmed, 335 vetted away, 0 real conflicts missed.

Operational context: aircraft on or near the runway surface at SFO/OAK/SJC are excluded from pairing (ground ops are not separation conflicts). Formation flight (matched velocity inside 0.7 NM) and parallel-final arrival pairs (both established on approach to the same field, runway-aligned) are annotated on the panel and not counted as separation losses - the 3 NM / 1000 ft standard does not govern those procedures.
